The Premise

Yaoyorozu Momo has had vivid dreams since she was 10. Some of a young woman in a haori resembling a monarch butterfly, surrounded by Wisteria. Others of a woman with fiery red hair and a gun in hand. There's often blood.

Yaomomo shares a soul with the deceased Natasha Romanov and Kochou Shinobu. The vestiges guide her to become a Pro Hero, with a few twists along the way. The former Insect Pillar even gets to reunite with the ever-aloof former Water Hashira, who's connected to the teen's seatmate.
